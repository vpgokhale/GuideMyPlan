Status

Status buttons sit under the wheel. While one is on, the wheel fills in its color instead of the task's, and that time counts as the status.
Number keys switch tasks, letter keys (Q, W, E…) turn statuses on and off, and Space pauses. Paused rows stop the clock and are not counted.
